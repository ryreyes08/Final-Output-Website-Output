Throughout the years Danda's Pizza in Angeles City has become just as popular with visitors and tourists as it is with native Kapampangan who know where to come for a street slice.

Established in 2005 by John Reese, who is originally from Naples, Italy, the birthplace of pizza, Danda's Pizza has been offering the classic Kapampangan slice for over 20 years. At first, we served our customers from our corner location in Sto. Domingo.
