The Heart of Danda’s Pizza

Our Story

Founded in Angeles City in 2005, Danda’s Pizza began with a simple dream: to bring people together through the love of good food. What started as a passion for authentic, freshly made pizza grew into a place where friends, families, and neighbors could gather, share stories, and create lasting memories. For us, pizza has always been more than just a meal—it is a way to bring people closer together.

What Makes Us Different?

At Danda’s Pizza, we believe that great pizza starts with great ingredients, patience, and a whole lot of passion. Every morning, our kitchen comes alive as we prepare our dough from scratch, giving it a full 24 hours to rest and develop its signature flavor and texture. Each pizza is carefully hand-stretched and baked in our custom wood-fired oven, creating a deliciously crispy crust with a soft, chewy center.

We take pride in every pizza we serve, combining traditional techniques with our dedication to quality. From the first bite to the very last slice, we want every guest to experience the warmth, freshness, and flavor that make Danda’s Pizza special.

Meet the Team

Behind every great pizza is a team of people who care deeply about what they do. Danda’s Pizza is built on a shared passion for cooking, a commitment to quality, and the belief that the best meals are made with heart.

Our team works together every day to prepare fresh dough, craft delicious pizzas, and welcome every guest with friendly service. Every recipe we make and every pizza we serve reflects our dedication to giving our community something worth coming back for.

You're Family Here

At Danda's Pizza, you aren't just a customer—you're family. We are dedicated to providing excellent service, a welcoming atmosphere, and a meal that leaves you smiling every single time.
